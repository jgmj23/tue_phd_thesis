\section{Securing the Edge AI Data Path}

The deployment questions raised for isolated post-quantum primitives in
Chapter~\ref{ch:dpu-pqc} are tested most sharply not on isolated primitives, but
inside complete application pipelines. The edge-AI setting examined here involves a
pipeline where post-quantum protection, image I/O from sensors, and GPU-based inference
all compete for the same datapath. In such scenarios, the theoretical efficiency of a PQC
algorithm matters less than how gracefully its offload degrades when it must coexist
with real application workloads. Pipeline-level offload therefore tests whether the
DPU and PQC mechanisms of the preceding chapter remain useful within a broader,
contention-prone application workflow.
